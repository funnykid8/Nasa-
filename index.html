POLAR SITE NOTES
  • High Sun elevation generally reduces long shadows.
  • Terrain can block sunlight even when the Sun is geometrically above the horizon.
  • Near the poles, illumination varies strongly with terrain and time.
  • NASA polar-illumination products model terrain-based lighting over long time periods.
